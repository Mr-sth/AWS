## Understand the Four Tools

### AWS Pricing Calculator
Estimate what your planned AWS architecture is likely to cost **before** you deploy it.

- **Example:** Estimate monthly costs for EC2, S3, and a database in a selected Region.

### AWS Cost Explorer
Explore and analyze your AWS spending using historical and current cost data.

- **Example:** Find out why your EC2 bill increased last month.

### AWS Budgets
Set spending or usage limits and receive alerts when actual or forecasted costs reach your thresholds.

- **Example:** Receive an alert when your monthly cost reaches $100.

### AWS Trusted Advisor
Get recommendations and checks relating to cost optimization, security, performance, fault tolerance, and service limits.

- **Example:** Identify underutilized resources that might be costing money unnecessarily.
