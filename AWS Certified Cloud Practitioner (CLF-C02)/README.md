# ☁️ AWS Cloud Practitioner — Questions & Notes

## Question 1

**A company currently owns physical servers in its data center. It wants to avoid purchasing additional servers whenever application demand increases.**

**Which AWS Cloud benefit best addresses this requirement?**

* A. Trade fixed expense for variable expense
* **B. Stop guessing capacity**
* C. Increase speed and agility
* D. Benefit from economies of scale

### ✅ Answer

**B. Stop guessing capacity**

### 📝 Notes

* Traditional IT requires you to **predict future capacity**.
* AWS lets you **increase or decrease resources based on demand**.
* **Too few servers →** performance problems.
* **Too many servers →** wasted money.

> 💡 **Remember:** Stop guessing capacity = **Adjust resources as demand changes.**

### Question 2

* A. Go global in minutes
* B. Benefit from massive economies of scale

> 💡 **Remember:** Fixed → Variable = **Buy → Pay as you use**

### Question 8

A company used to purchase enough physical servers to handle its **maximum expected workload**. Most of the year, many of those servers were sitting unused.

After moving to AWS, the company can add resources when demand increases and remove them when demand decreases.

**Which benefit is the best match?**

* A. Trade fixed expense for variable expense
* **B. Stop guessing capacity**
* C. Benefit from massive economies of scale
* D. Go global in minutes

### ✅ Answer

**B. Stop guessing capacity**

### Notes

* The company no longer needs to **buy for maximum expected demand**.
* Resources can be **added when demand increases**.
* Resources can be **removed when demand decreases**.

> 💡 **Remember:** 📊 Changing capacity with demand = **Stop guessing capacity**
